Look Up

Prototype 1 · October 2026
  • Website
  • Thesis 1, Fall 2026
  • October 2026
  • Open the site ↗
  • Presentation ↗

What happens when the ordinary city furniture we pass every day, the things that hold our memories, is woken up again through a pigeon's eyes? This project continues the 256 book's conversation about pigeons, people and the city, and this time it focuses more on each person's own point of view. When we actively look for pigeons, the scenery our eyes sweep past, which we never paid attention to, becomes our memory of this city together with the pigeons themselves.

Made with p5.js, the work encourages viewers to interact with the website actively, by looking up and by making the framing gesture of taking a photo, and to leave their own traces on the site: the feathers and memories they leave as they fly over the city.

As an invitation to the website, the second part of this prototype begins with a “pigeon feather” and ends with a feather that holds the user's own story. When viewers actively look for the traces of the city, they also become part of those traces.

The paper feather 6

The website is one part of the prototype; this paper is the other. It brings people to the website. It imitates the pigeon feathers we often come across on the street, so it can blend into the city naturally. Someone picks up the feather, opens it, and finds the website and its interaction.

Paper feathers in the city 24

Paper feathers left where pigeon feathers turn up in New York: on railings, grates, hydrants, planters and a bike seat, waiting for someone to pick one up and open the website.
