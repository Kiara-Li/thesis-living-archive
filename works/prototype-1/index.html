The paper feather 6

The website is one part of the prototype; this paper is the other. It brings people to the website. It imitates the pigeon feathers we often come across on the street, so it can blend into the city naturally — someone picks up the feather, opens it, and finds the website and its interaction.

Paper feathers in the city 24

Paper feathers left where pigeon feathers turn up in New York — railings, grates, hydrants, planters, a bike seat — waiting for someone to pick one up and open the website.
